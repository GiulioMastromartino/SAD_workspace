The omni's own 1e-12 is a choice, not a constraint

Sweeping the omni's tolerance from 1e-4 to 1e-16 on basicOrbit_LEO (600 s): it takes 6000 steps — one per tick — at every single tolerance, and the resulting trajectory is bit-identical. Tighter only raises the Taylor order: 2.6 ms build / 18.3 ms run at 1e-4, 6.6 ms / 27.8 ms at 1e-12, 10.4 ms / 36.7 ms at 1e-16. So the shipped setting costs the omni 1.52× what 1e-4 would, for output that does not change.

Consequences

  • RK4 @ dt=0.1 is not an unfair baseline; it is the cheapest stock choice that is already exact. Basilisk ships tolerance-controlled integrators (svIntegratorRKF45, svIntegratorRKF78, per-state rel/abs tolerance, plus adaptive RK1–RK13 and a free Butcher-table factory), and Basilisk's own demos use RKF78/RKF45 where accuracy matters. Swapping them in at dt=0.1 buys zero accuracy and costs 2.17–2.18× (RKF45) or 5.24–5.35× (RKF78). The headline would read 5.8–7.0× and 14.1–17.3× instead of 2.7–3.2× — those numbers are available, they are just not more honest.
  • On gravity-only legs the omni is the more accurate arm at any step. RK4 needs dt ≲ 0.4 s to reach the same floor the omni holds at dt=15 s, so a genuinely accuracy-matched speed comparison would have to refine the stock side, never the omni.
  • On drag legs the ordering flips. The omni's first-order term is ≈ 7.4e-5 · dt metres — 7.4e-6 m at dt=0.1, and it is the only arm below fourth order. It crosses under RK4's error only near dt ≈ 7 s, which is why the demo's own coarse steps look favourable to the mirror and why the hijack leg needed a 1e3 m tolerance.
  • A correction to how we described that drag residual. It was recorded as a first-order-in-dt density-sampling difference shared with stock ("stock samples it across RK4 substeps"). That attribution does not survive this measurement: stock RK4 is cleanly fourth-order and, at dt=0.1, bit-identical to RKF78 @ 1e-14. The first-order term is entirely omni-side, in the mirrored drag path. The magnitude and the r ∝ dt behaviour we documented are right; the mechanism was not.
